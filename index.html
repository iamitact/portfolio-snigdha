01 / About

The short version

I finished my B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at East West University in 2024. Most of my hands-on work so far has come from coursework and academic projects — analysing circuits, simulating them in MATLAB, drafting designs in AutoCAD, and writing up what actually happened rather than what was supposed to happen. I am methodical about documentation, comfortable coordinating a project team, and I would rather ask one more question than hand over work I am not sure of.
