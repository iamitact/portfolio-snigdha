02 — Selected work

Projects

2024 · EEE 402 Control System · East West University

Elevator Controller using PLC

A controller for a four-floor elevator, built on a PLC. The four call buttons and the four floor-position sensors are each encoded into a 2-bit value, and a magnitude comparator decides between them: button above the car means up, below means down, equal means stop. A load-weighing sensor sits above all of it and forces a stop the moment the car is overloaded, whatever the other inputs say.

We compared a finite-state-machine design against the magnitude comparator and chose the comparator — fewer variables, no flip-flops and lower cost for a building this size.

  • PLC
  • Ladder logic

  • Digital logic

Research paper · Department of EEE, East West University

Wearable Women Safety Device

A NodeMCU-based wristband that monitors heart rate, blood-oxygen saturation and body temperature and streams them to a Blynk IoT dashboard, so a guardian can check on the wearer remotely. An emergency button sends an SMS through the GSM module carrying live GPS coordinates to pre-set contacts. The whole circuit was simulated in Proteus before the prototype was built.

Against a requirement of 2%, the oxygen-saturation sensor measured to within 1.5%. Co-authored with Md. Shafi Uddin Bhuan.
